Northstar Grid Analytics Ltd.
Capacity Update
Published July 22, 2026

Northstar entered the second half of 2026 with demand for grid-monitoring installations above its internal plan.

The primary constraint on new customer deployments is a shortage of high-voltage transformers. Management expects supplier lead times to improve during the first half of 2027, but the schedule remains uncertain.

Software configuration capacity is sufficient for the current backlog. The company has not changed its product roadmap or customer-acceptance criteria.
